Study Companion from Real Notes

Adaptive Learning and Knowledge Support

A focused study experience that uses your material as its source, makes supporting evidence visible, and helps you learn from practice.

The opportunity

Problem background

Students often revise from lectures, notes, and short PDFs that may not be easy to understand. A generic chatbot can answer from broad knowledge even when the answer is absent from the study material. Learners need a way to identify what their notes actually say, what remains unclear, and which claims are supported by the source.

What we are building

Problem statement

Build an AI-powered study companion that turns user-provided learning material into an interactive tutoring session. Students select one topic, upload or paste their notes, ask questions, inspect supporting evidence, and practice with targeted feedback. This prototype focuses on one academic topic and a small set of learning materials rather than a full learning-management system.

A simple, inspectable learning loop

Expected solution

Use local AI to interpret the material while keeping the source visible and making gaps explicit.

Core requirements

  • Upload or paste notes, including text-based PDFs, for one selected topic.
  • Answer questions from supplied material and show identifiable supporting evidence.
  • Generate a relevant practice question and evaluate the student's response.
  • Explain incorrect answers and provide actionable hints.
  • Clearly say when the notes do not contain enough information.
  • Use a local Ollama model for interpretation and learning support.

Demonstration checklist

  • Load sample notes for one topic.
  • Ask a supported question and inspect the source lines.
  • Submit an incorrect quiz answer and review the explanation.
  • Ask an unsupported question and confirm the limitation is clear.

Possible directions

  • Explain supplied notes, documents, diagrams, or study material.
  • Generate practice activities, feedback, or learning plans.
  • Answer questions with identifiable evidence from the supplied material.
  • Adapt explanations to the learner's progress.

Grounded by design

Relevant source lines are retrieved before an answer is generated. The local model is instructed to stay within those lines, and quiz answers are checked against an exact quote from the notes.
